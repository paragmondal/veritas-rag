ACME TECHNOLOGIES INC.
BOARD OF DIRECTORS CHARTER OF THE AUDIT COMMITTEE
Adopted by the Board of Directors: February 15, 2024
Amended and Restated: January 18, 2025

I. PURPOSE OF THE COMMITTEE
The Audit Committee (the "Committee") of the Board of Directors (the "Board") of Acme Technologies Inc. ("Acme") is appointed by the Board to assist in monitoring and overseeing:
1. The integrity of the financial statements and internal accounting controls of the Company;
2. The Company's compliance with legal, ethical, and regulatory requirements;
3. The qualifications, engagement, independence, and performance of the Company's independent registered public accounting firm;
4. The performance of the Company's internal audit function; and
5. Enterprise risk management, including cybersecurity risk and automated controls.

II. COMMITTEE COMPOSITION AND INDEPENDENCE
1. Size: The Committee shall consist of at least three (3) members of the Board.
2. Independence: Each member of the Committee must meet the independence requirements of the applicable stock exchange and Rule 10A-3 under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as determined by the Board.
3. Financial Literacy: Each member must be financially literate at the time of appointment, capable of reading and understanding fundamental financial statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements.
4. Financial Expert: At least one (1) member of the Committee must qualify as an "audit committee financial expert" as defined under Item 407(d)(5) of Regulation S-K.

III. MEETINGS, PROCEDURES, AND QUORUM
1. Meeting Cadence: The Committee shall meet at least four (4) times per fiscal year on a quarterly basis, prior to the public dissemination of quarterly earnings results, and at such additional times as the Committee Chairperson or any two members deem necessary.
2. Quorum: A majority of the total number of members of the Committee shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business at any meeting.
3. Voting: The act of a majority of the Committee members present at a meeting at which a quorum is present shall be the act of the Committee. The Committee may also act by unanimous written consent in lieu of a meeting.
4. Executive Sessions: The Committee shall meet privately in executive session at least once each quarter with the independent auditor, the Chief Financial Officer, and the Director of Internal Audit.

IV. PRIMARY DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
1. Independent Auditor Oversight: Direct responsibility for the appointment, compensation, retention, evaluation, and termination of the independent auditor. Pre-approval of all audit and permitted non-audit services.
2. Financial Reporting Review: Review and discuss with management and the independent auditor the annual audited financial statements on Form 10-K and quarterly financial statements on Form 10-Q prior to filing with the SEC.
3. Whistleblower Procedures: Establish, maintain, and oversee formal procedures for the confidential, anonymous submission by employees of concerns regarding questionable accounting, internal controls, or auditing matters.
