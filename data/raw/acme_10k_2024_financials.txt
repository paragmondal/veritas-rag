ACME TECHNOLOGIES INC.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR FISCAL YEAR 2024

Acme Technologies Inc. ("Acme", the "Company", "we", or "our") reported total consolidated revenue of $4,250.0 million for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, representing an increase of $450.0 million or 11.8% compared to total revenue of $3,800.0 million for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023. 

Consolidated gross profit for fiscal year 2024 was $2,601.0 million, resulting in a consolidated gross margin of 61.2%, compared to gross profit of $2,280.0 million and a gross margin of 60.0% in fiscal year 2023. Operating income for fiscal year 2024 totaled $935.0 million, yielding an operating margin of 22.0%, compared to operating income of $798.0 million in fiscal year 2023. Net income for fiscal year 2024 was $745.0 million, resulting in diluted earnings per share (EPS) of $3.45 across 215.9 million weighted-average diluted common shares outstanding. Cash and cash equivalents stood at $1,180.0 million as of December 31, 2024.

ITEM 7. SEGMENT PERFORMANCE AND REVENUE BREAKDOWN

Our business is organized into three reportable segments: Cloud Platform Services, Enterprise Hardware Systems, and Professional & Advisory Services.

1. Cloud Platform Services:
Revenue in Cloud Platform Services was $2,100.0 million in fiscal year 2024, contributing 49.4% of total consolidated revenue. This represents year-over-year revenue expansion of 20.0% from $1,750.0 million in fiscal year 2023. Segment gross profit was $1,428.0 million, achieving a segment gross margin of 68.0%. Growth was driven by enterprise adoption of our flagship cloud data platform, TitanScale.

2. Enterprise Hardware Systems:
Revenue in Enterprise Hardware Systems was $1,400.0 million in fiscal year 2024, accounting for 32.9% of total consolidated revenue, compared to $1,375.0 million in fiscal year 2023 (1.8% growth). Segment gross profit was $728.0 million, yielding a segment gross margin of 52.0%. Growth reflected ongoing replacement cycles for edge telemetry appliances.

3. Professional & Advisory Services:
Revenue in Professional & Advisory Services was $750.0 million in fiscal year 2024, representing 17.7% of total consolidated revenue, compared to $675.0 million in fiscal year 2023 (11.1% growth). Segment gross profit was $445.0 million, corresponding to a segment gross margin of 59.3%. Growth was fueled by cloud migration integration consulting.

ITEM 7. OPERATING EXPENSES, R&D, AND WORKFORCE HEADCOUNT

Operating expenses for fiscal year 2024 were as follows:
- Research and Development (R&D): $820.0 million in fiscal year 2024, representing 19.3% of total consolidated revenue, compared to $722.0 million in fiscal year 2023. R&D investments focused on next-generation vector query acceleration and distributed edge consensus engines.
- Sales and Marketing (S&M): $646.0 million in fiscal year 2024 (15.2% of revenue), compared to $570.0 million in fiscal year 2023.
- General and Administrative (G&A): $200.0 million in fiscal year 2024 (4.7% of revenue), compared to $190.0 million in fiscal year 2023.

Workforce and Headcount:
As of December 31, 2024, Acme employed 14,200 full-time employees globally, an increase of 1,100 employees from 13,100 full-time employees as of December 31, 2023. The engineering and technical staff comprised 58% of total headcount (8,236 employees), while customer success and enterprise sales accounted for 27% (3,834 employees), and general administration comprised 15% (2,130 employees). Capital expenditures for property, equipment, and server clusters totaled $310.0 million in fiscal year 2024.
