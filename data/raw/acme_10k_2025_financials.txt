ACME TECHNOLOGIES INC.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR FISCAL YEAR 2025

Acme Technologies Inc. ("Acme", the "Company", "we", or "our") reported total consolidated revenue of $5,185.0 million for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, representing an increase of $935.0 million or 22.0% compared to total revenue of $4,250.0 million for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024.

Consolidated gross profit for fiscal year 2025 was $3,318.4 million, resulting in a consolidated gross margin of 64.0%, representing an expansion of 280 basis points compared to gross profit of $2,601.0 million and gross margin of 61.2% in fiscal year 2024. Operating income for fiscal year 2025 reached $1,244.4 million, yielding an operating margin of 24.0%, compared to operating income of $935.0 million and an operating margin of 22.0% in fiscal year 2024. Net income for fiscal year 2025 was $995.5 million, an increase of 33.6% compared to $745.0 million in fiscal year 2024, resulting in diluted earnings per share (EPS) of $4.55 compared to $3.45 in fiscal year 2024. Cash and cash equivalents totaled $1,640.0 million as of December 31, 2025.

ITEM 7. SEGMENT PERFORMANCE AND REVENUE BREAKDOWN

Our business is organized into three reportable segments: Cloud Platform Services, Enterprise Hardware Systems, and Professional & Advisory Services.

1. Cloud Platform Services:
Revenue in Cloud Platform Services rose to $2,850.0 million in fiscal year 2025, contributing 55.0% of total consolidated revenue. This represents strong year-over-year revenue growth of 35.7% from $2,100.0 million in fiscal year 2024. Segment gross profit was $2,052.0 million, achieving a segment gross margin of 72.0%, representing an expansion of 400 basis points from 68.0% in fiscal year 2024. Growth was accelerated by enterprise subscription expansion for TitanScale and new generative indexing microservices.

2. Enterprise Hardware Systems:
Revenue in Enterprise Hardware Systems was $1,450.0 million in fiscal year 2025, accounting for 28.0% of total consolidated revenue, compared to $1,400.0 million in fiscal year 2024 (growth of 3.57%). Segment gross profit was $725.0 million, yielding a segment gross margin of 50.0%, compared to 52.0% in fiscal year 2024. Margin compression reflected higher component sourcing costs.

3. Professional & Advisory Services:
Revenue in Professional & Advisory Services was $885.0 million in fiscal year 2025, representing 17.1% of total consolidated revenue, compared to $750.0 million in fiscal year 2024 (growth of 18.0%). Segment gross profit was $541.4 million, achieving a segment gross margin of 61.17%, up from 59.3% in fiscal year 2024.

ITEM 7. OPERATING EXPENSES, R&D, AND WORKFORCE HEADCOUNT

Operating expenses for fiscal year 2025 were as follows:
- Research and Development (R&D): $1,050.0 million in fiscal year 2025, representing 20.3% of total consolidated revenue, compared to $820.0 million (19.3% of revenue) in fiscal year 2024, representing an increase of $230.0 million or 28.0%. R&D investments prioritized real-time hybrid retrieval kernels, neural compression, and enterprise compliance architectures.
- Sales and Marketing (S&M): $780.0 million in fiscal year 2025 (15.0% of revenue), compared to $646.0 million in fiscal year 2024.
- General and Administrative (G&A): $244.0 million in fiscal year 2025 (4.7% of revenue), compared to $200.0 million in fiscal year 2024.

Workforce and Headcount:
As of December 31, 2025, Acme employed 16,100 full-time employees globally, an addition of 1,900 employees or 13.4% growth from 14,200 full-time employees as of December 31, 2024. Technical and product personnel represented 60% of total headcount (9,660 employees), customer operations and sales represented 26% (4,186 employees), and general corporate administration comprised 14% (2,254 employees). Capital expenditures reached $425.0 million in fiscal year 2025.
